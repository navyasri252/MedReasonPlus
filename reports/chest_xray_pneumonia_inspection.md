## 1. Dataset Overview

Source: Chest X-Ray Images (Pneumonia) — Kermany et al., Kaggle.
Images are JPEG chest X-rays split into NORMAL and PNEUMONIA classes.
PNEUMONIA images are further identified by filename as bacterial or viral.
